> SAMPLE – illustrative, not authoritative. Based on widely taught requirements-engineering practice.

## Characteristics of a good requirement
Each requirement should be necessary, unambiguous, verifiable, feasible,
consistent with other requirements and traceable to its source. It should
state one need, name the actor, and avoid vague words such as "fast",
"secure" or "user-friendly" without a measurable criterion.

## Non-functional requirements
Quality requirements must state a measurable target and the condition under
which it is measured, for example a response time at a given load.

## Traceability
Every requirement should link back to the stakeholder statement or document
it came from, and forward to the tests that verify it.
