## Identification before relationship
A customer must be identified and their identity verified using reliable,
independent documents or data before an account is opened or credit is granted.

## Risk-based due diligence
Customers must be assigned a risk rating. Higher-risk customers require
enhanced due diligence, including source-of-funds information and senior
management approval before onboarding.

## Ongoing monitoring
Customer information must be kept up to date through periodic review, with
higher-risk customers reviewed more frequently. Unusual activity must be
escalated to the designated compliance function.

## Records
Identification records and due-diligence evidence must be retained for the
period set in the applicable record-retention obligation and made available to
authorised reviewers on request.
