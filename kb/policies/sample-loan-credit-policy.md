## Eligibility and affordability
Every personal loan application must pass an eligibility check before any
credit decision. The check covers identity verification status, minimum age
under the institution's own rules, and an affordability assessment that
compares declared income against existing obligations.

## Credit decision authority
Automated approval is allowed only when the applicant's credit score meets the
institution's configured cut-off and the requested amount is within the
automated-decision limit set by the credit committee. All other applications
must be routed to a human underwriter. Overrides of an automated decision
require a documented reason and the underwriter's identity.

## Adverse action and explanation
When an application is declined, the applicant must receive the principal
reasons for the decision in plain language. Reasons must be traceable to the
data and rules used in the decision.

## Record keeping
Application data, decision inputs, the decision outcome and any overrides must
be retained for the period defined in the institution's record-retention
schedule and must be retrievable for audit.
