> SAMPLE – illustrative template. Templates are structure, not evidence: they are
> not in config.KB_EVIDENCE_TYPES, so they are never cited by the agents.

## Outline
1. Introduction - purpose, scope, definitions, references.
2. Overall description - product perspective, users, constraints, assumptions.
3. Specific requirements - functional, non-functional, interfaces, data.
4. Appendices - traceability matrix, open issues.
