> SAMPLE – illustrative, not authoritative. General software-engineering guidance, not a regulatory requirement.

## Matching models to project characteristics
Stable, well-understood requirements suit plan-driven models such as
Waterfall. Heavy verification and validation needs suit the V-Model.
High technical uncertainty suits risk-driven Spiral development. Frequent
change and the need for early user feedback suit Agile. High security needs
combined with continuous delivery suit DevSecOps.

## Regulated delivery
Regulated financial systems usually need documented approvals, traceability
from requirements to tests, and evidence of security testing, whatever
delivery model is used. Hybrid models are common: iterative delivery with
formal verification gates.
